Macbeth Revison

Overview

Macbeth is a tragedy by William Shakespeare. It explores ambition, power, guilt and the consequences of seeking control.

Characters

Macbeth

Macbeth is a brave warrior whose ambition leads him to commit terrible actions.

Lady Macbeth

Lady Macbeth is ambitious and manipulative, encouraging Macbeth to pursue power.

Banquo

Banquo is Macbeth's friend who represents morality and loyalty.

The Witches

The Witches introduce the supernatural elements and influence Macbeth's decisions.
