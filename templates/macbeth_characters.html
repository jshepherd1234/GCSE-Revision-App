Macbeth

Role in the play

Macbeth is the tragic hero of the play. He begins as a respected and courageous soldier, but his ambition and the witches' prophecies lead him towards violence, betrayl, and tyranny.

Character development

Macbeth starts as a noble warrior fighting for king and country, but as the play progresses, negins to succumb to his ambition and allow the supernatural to control him

Key themes

  • Ambition
  • Power
  • Guilt

Key quotations

"disdaining fortune with his brandished steel which smoked with bloody execution"

Lady Macbeth

Role in the play

Lady Macbeth encourages Macbeth to pursue his ambition of taking the crown. She initially appears determined and ruthless but later becomes overwhelmed by guilt.

Charcter development

She begins as the more dominant partner, but gradually loses control whilst Macbeth becomes increasingly independant and violent.

Key themes:

  • Ambition
  • Guilt
  • Power
  • Gender

Key quotations:

  • "Too full of the milk of human kindness to cath the nearest way"
  • "Look like the innocent flowe, but be the serpent under't"
  • "Out, damned spot"

Banquo

Role in the play

Banquo is Macbeth's friend and fellow soldier. Unlike Macbeth, he is cautious about the witches' prophecies and represents loyalty and self-control.

Charcter development

Banquo remains suspicious of the suoernatural and acts as a contrast to Macbeth's willingness to pursue power through violence.

Key themes:

  • Loyalty
  • Fate
  • Morality
  • The supernatural

Key quotations:

  • "My noble partner seems rapt withal"
  • "The instruments of darkness tell us truths"

The Witches

Role in the play

The witches introduce the supernatural and tempt Macbeth with prophecies about his future. Their language creates uncertainty and confusion.

Importance

They create uncertainty through misleading laanguage, but they do not directly force Macbeth to act.

Key themes:

  • The supernatural
  • Fate
  • Appearence vs reality
  • Ambition

Key quotations

  • "Fair is foul, and foul is fair"
  • "Something wicked this way comes"
