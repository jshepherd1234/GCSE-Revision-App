Learn the key characters, their roles, themes and quotations.

Macbeth

Macbeth is the tragic hero of the play. He begins as a respected and courageous soldier, but his ambition and the witches' prophecies lead him towards violence, betrayl, and tyranny.

Key themes

  • Ambition
  • Power
  • Guilt
Key quotations

"disdaining fortune with his brandished steel which smoked with bloody execution"

Lady Macbeth

Lady Macbeth encourages Macbeth to pursue his ambition of taking the crown. She initially appears determined and ruthless but later becomes overwhelmed by guilt.

Banquo

Banquo is Macbeth's friend and fellow soldier. Unlike Macbeth, he is cautious about the witches' prophecies and represents loyalty and self-control.

The Witches

The witches introduce the supernatural and tempt Macbeth with prophecies about his future Their language creates uncertainty and confusion.
